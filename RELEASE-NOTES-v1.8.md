# QuizDock v1.8

- TV displays now identify the contestant who buzzed, show the submitted response during judging, and show both that response and the correct answer during the reveal.
- Final Jeopardy announces a winner or co-winners once all wagers have been resolved.
- Missing Final Jeopardy responses are now scored as incorrect when time expires or the host reveals early.
- Added the supplied Daily Double, timeout, round-over, and Final Jeopardy sound effects.
- Added a rough progress bar and percentage-bearing generation updates.
- Local clue validation failures now trigger up to three targeted single-clue replacements before a full game retry.
- Media clues remain text-first and render optional images, audio, or video with native browser elements rather than unsafe arbitrary iframes.
